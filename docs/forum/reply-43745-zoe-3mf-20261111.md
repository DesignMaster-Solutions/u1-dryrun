@Zoe_M, the 22 hour plate is @Mr.Borispol's and I only have the G-code he shared in post 12, not his project. So I made a small project that shows the same collision, in case it saves the engineers time while his file is on the way:

https://github.com/DesignMaster-Solutions/u1-dryrun/raw/main/examples/tower-right-edge-orca-2.4.1.3mf

It is a two-colour sign, 15 layers, default U1 0.20 mm profile, two Snapmaker PLA, prime tower at X236.5 Y100. Sliced with 2.4.1, 8 of the 15 tool changes lower the new head at the dock right after `T0` or `T1` (first one: line 4525, `G1 Z.45 F30000`) and then go straight to the tower. On the U1 firmware that gives 3 travels through the part at layer height and one over the finished top, 91 mm long.

Two controls with the same model and profile: 2.4.1 with the tower 5.6 mm to the left (X230.853) writes the detour and has no such travel, and 2.4.0 with the tower at X236.5 is clean.

I sliced it from the command line on macOS and have not opened it in the Orca window or printed it. The table and the lines are also in issue #1168, where the engineer asked for a 3mf today: https://github.com/Snapmaker/OrcaSlicer/issues/1168#issuecomment-6791877755
