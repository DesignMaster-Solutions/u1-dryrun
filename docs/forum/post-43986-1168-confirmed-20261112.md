Update, 12 November: Snapmaker has confirmed the second finding as well.

This is the one from the last post: after a tool change, Snapmaker Orca 2.4.1 lowers the new head while it is still at its dock and then sends it to the prime tower at layer height. Their developer took the KOI Fish project that Mr.Borispol shared and wrote today that with the tower at the edge of the bed the Z axis goes down first and then moves to the tower, which is incorrect, and that the December version of Snapmaker Orca will fix it: https://github.com/Snapmaker/OrcaSlicer/issues/1168#issuecomment-6814171273

So both slicer problems this tool has traced from owners' real files are now accepted by Snapmaker: the final purge travel (#1149, fix announced for the November version) and this one (#1168, December).

Until the fix is out:

- Keep the prime tower away from the bed edge. The exact rule I measured on the two projects: the tower with its ribs and brim, plus 2 mm, has to be inside the bed on the right and at the back. Moving it 1 mm on one plate and 6 mm on the other took the count from 94 and 222 tool changes to 0.
- To know if a file you already sliced has it, without Docker and in a second: `python3 dock_drops.py yourfile.gcode` (https://github.com/DesignMaster-Solutions/u1-dryrun/blob/main/tools/dock_drops.py).

What I have not done: no build with either fix exists yet, so nothing is replayed. When they are out I will slice the same projects again and post the counts here and on the two issues.
